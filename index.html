A word of strategy

Your marketplace listing
is not your brand.

Before a buyer completes a purchase on any marketplace, a significant number of them quietly open a new tab and search for your brand directly. What they find — or don't find — determines whether they buy.

81%
of shoppers research a brand online before buying
Even if they first discovered the product on a marketplace, they look you up independently before committing.
73%
of buyers say a brand website increases their purchase confidence
Especially for premium and gifting purchases — buyers want to feel they're buying from a brand, not just a listing.
2×
higher likelihood of repeat purchase when a brand website exists
Marketplace buyers who find a brand website are far more likely to return directly — bypassing the marketplace and its fees entirely.
Our recommendation
You don't need a full e-commerce store to start. A brand website — your story, your products, your contact — is enough to earn the trust that converts a marketplace browser into a buyer.
For a premium gifting brand like Wrapover, the website is your corporate gifting pitch deck, your wholesale catalogue, and your credibility certificate — all in one place. A ₹1,50,000 brand website is the minimum that reflects the quality of what you sell.
